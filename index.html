Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatSend ("WhatSend", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Dihyah Raj, collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our application and services (the "Service"). WhatSend helps merchants send and manage WhatsApp messages to their customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Merchant account information — your name, email address, business details, and connected store information (such as your Shopify store domain).
  • WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) data — the WABA, phone number(s), and account identifiers you connect to WhatSend through Meta's Embedded Signup flow.
  • Message data — the content and metadata of messages and message templates you send or receive through the Service, including delivery and read status.
  • End-customer contact information — phone numbers and related contact details that you, the merchant, choose to message through the Service.
  • Technical and usage data — log data, IP address, browser/device information, and usage activity needed to operate and secure the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including sending and routing WhatsApp messages on your behalf.
  • To create, manage, and submit message templates to Meta on your behalf.
  • To provide analytics, delivery reporting, and customer support.
  • To protect the security and integrity of the Service and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Meta's platform requirements.

3. WhatsApp Business Platform & Meta

WhatSend operates as a technology provider on the WhatsApp Business Platform. To deliver messages, information is processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates in accordance with Meta's own terms and policies. By using WhatSend, you also agree to comply with the WhatsApp Business Terms of Service and applicable Meta platform policies. The connection between your WhatsApp Business Account and WhatSend is established by you through Meta's Embedded Signup, and your account remains owned by you.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With Meta / WhatsApp — to deliver and manage messages and templates as you direct.
  • With service providers — such as hosting and infrastructure partners who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time, after which we will delete or anonymize it unless we are legally required to retain it.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption of data in transit and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
